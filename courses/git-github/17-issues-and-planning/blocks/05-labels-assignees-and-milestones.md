type: Explain
title: Labels, assignees, and milestones
---

A label categorizes an issue: `bug`, `documentation`, `help wanted`, `blocked`. Labels are shared by the repository, so they make filtering and reporting possible. They do not grant permission and they do not change code.

An assignee is the person expected to do the work. Assigning an issue signals ownership; it does not give the assignee write access. An issue can have several assignees, though one clear owner is usually easier to follow.

A milestone groups issues toward a target, such as a release or a sprint. It is a planning bucket with an optional due date. Closing the milestone does not close its issues automatically.

These fields are only useful when the team agrees on what they mean. A label that everyone applies differently is noise. A milestone with no scope is a date without a plan. Use a small vocabulary, document it, and remove labels that no longer mean anything.

Triage is the act of applying those fields: confirm the report, remove duplicates, ask for missing details, set priority, assign an owner, and decide whether the issue is accepted, deferred, or closed.
